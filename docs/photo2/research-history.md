The older `photo-2-reconstruction-v2` restarted from an older master base; it was
not a continuation of every first reconstruction experiment. V3 continues from
v2. Earlier image-to-pattern and `pattern` branches, and the separate
[bead_map](https://github.com/rharriszzz/bead_map) and
[hsv_tools](https://github.com/rharriszzz/hsv_tools) repositories, retain useful
experiments but did not deliver the final pattern. The retrospective records their
ancestry precisely. A recent commit or higher round number does not imply every
technique superseded all prior methods successfully.

## Deliberately historical only

Large masks, candidate inventories, whole-pipeline optimizers, raw graph/ledger
archives, native diagnostic atlases, rejected camera/light searches, previous
presentation epochs and failed synthetic illustrations stay on research. In
particular, R346's shadow-contaminated local-reference experiment and the visibility
example's initial rear-surface leak are preserved as rejected results. They are
not the source of the promoted measurements or figures.

R345/R346 rely on coupled earlier sources, data and include paths. Their complete
pinned reproduction instructions are more useful than a broken partial tree.
The original source, proofs and historical tests remain unmodified. No scientific
labels, camera, geometry, light or active graph changed during curation.

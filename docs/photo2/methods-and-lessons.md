# Methods worth carrying forward

[Overview](overview.md) · [Research history](research-history.md)

The most productive late route was a trustworthy photographic graph plus an
incomplete color word, followed by targeted pigment/identity review. A full
optical inversion was not necessary to ask which repeat predicted separated
observations. Rendering became useful as a distinct deliverable with explicit
construction choices.

## Practices to reuse

1. **Choose the representation from the claim.** A feature, distinct body,
   lattice site, string index and pattern prediction are different entities.
   Preserve source pixels, aliases, authority and unknowns through each relation.
2. **Establish a small real-data reference early.** Trusted local neighbor
   triangles and separated patches expose row-switch and ownership errors better
   than a plausible aggregate detection count. Apply eligibility by inference
   role before inspecting motif agreement.
3. **Compare joint hypotheses.** R323's overlapping row alternatives improved the
   bounded interior reference; R326 showed that individually supported edges
   can still lack a simultaneous whole-hypothesis witness. Exact graph equations
   check bookkeeping, while photographs check the chosen relations.
4. **Gate scaling on known-answer recovery.** R301's accurate generator replayed
   truth, while inverse searches missed known geometry even with known owners.
   That is a search/evaluator diagnostic, not proof of physical impossibility.
5. **Protect independent correctness.** Stop adoption when a score improves but
   essential body coverage, topology or nonintersection regresses. R263's count
   target lost dark supports; R301 improved highlights while omitting material.
6. **Audit actual parameter activity and search.** R311 exposed missed basins;
   R319 exposed inactive fields and coupled responses. Serialized parameters and
   many trials do not demonstrate effective exploration or optimality.
7. **Preserve earlier predictions when extending a fit.** R297's global station
   normalization and phase changes regressed a useful local fit before new
   information justified the tradeoff. An archived image is not a live constraint.
8. **Keep a current claim register beside immutable history.** A compact table of
   claim, premises, strongest counterexample and next discriminating observation
   keeps failed epochs useful and prevents solved questions from returning.

Simple owned-material sampling was a valuable baseline, but the fixed 73.5
blackness threshold and pixel offsets are photograph-specific. R340–R343 retain
real ownership failures. They are not promoted as a universal pigment classifier.
General illustration approval also must not become an explicit individual
pigment judgment; R343 corrected that authority error.

## Diagnose the failure before choosing more work

| Diagnosis | Discriminating evidence | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Search/numerical failure | A known representable answer evaluates better than the returned solution | Repair search, screening, units, bounds or active variables before scaling |
| Model/target inadequacy | A specific missing behavior or objective rewards incorrect task outputs | Change representation or target; finite failed search alone is insufficient proof |
| Measurement ambiguity | Distinct valid interpretations satisfy the same checks | Report alternatives and seek a view or observation that separates them |

These mechanisms can coexist. A lower residual does not diagnose them. Convenient
synthetic tests may only check implementation: in R231 the rejected old method
passed the same controls as its proposed repair. A useful failure fixture must
first expose the motivating failure.

## Evidence-quality abstention

Unreadable dark edge cues should abstain from pigment, counting, occupancy or
obstruction claims when their ownership is unusable. A visible black body need
not have a bright highlight; there is no blanket dark-bead purge. Do not discard
a sound contrary observation because it disagrees with the candidate.

R347 Q1 shows why the answer's basis matters: a selected yellow supported by the
reconstruction cannot validate that reconstruction. Withholding pigment differs
from uncertain identity and hypothetical graph deletion. R348 checks the last
operation on saved edges and preserves the same conditional result, but does not
replay local image inference or adopt a modified graph.

For future pattern projects, start with stable IDs, explicit local neighbors,
partial words and separated validation regions. For future physical rendering,
first audit source dimensions/packing and known-answer inversion, then fit shared
geometry and illumination with independent checks. Multiple overlapping views
and an authentic chart can be more informative than another threshold or optimizer.
These are recommendations, not a new campaign or measured time-saving claim.

# R311 — broader basins in the existing global parameterization

The maker authorizes testing whether one or two substantially incorrect existing
globals, or a coupled combination, explain the earliest material discrepancies
and the whole-necklace mismatch. This supersedes the R310 stopping restriction
for this experiment only. Preserve all prior science; no new bead geometry,
arbitrary local correction, Phase 3 or automatic R312.

1. Audit every saved R306–R309 trial, including the initial/cold R306 runs and
   native refinement. Report actual ranges, joint coverage, discrete families,
   starts, rejection reasons, repeated neighborhoods and frozen fields.
2. Use the unchanged R297 2337-body anchor, R306 2405-body primary and R306
   2733-body alternative as principal hypotheses. Keep their implementations,
   fields, counts, hands, topology and winding distinct. Retain R309 and both
   R306 hand-plus families as controls. R297 is not silently canonicalized:
   R306's conversion discards several of its successful inherited local fields.
3. Five broad pair maps: phase/registration, phase/elevation, roll/elevation,
   minor-radius/scale, tilt/elevation. Phase spans one full cycle. Each feasible
   joint vector is evaluated directly, regardless of intervening invalid states.
4. Scrambled Sobol designs explore phase/registration/elevation/roll and
   elevation/minor-radius/scale/axial in four dimensions. Most computation goes
   to new vectors, without finite-difference matrices or derivative gates.
5. Initial budget: 2400 cumulative wall seconds inside the single search process,
   including contacts, native optical measurements, first-error diagnostics,
   checkpointing and basin decisions. Up to 78% is allocated to broad discovery;
   remaining time refines distinct feasible basins with joint proposals. Final
   dense evaluation, verification, plotting and delivery are separately timed.
   Budget exhaustion is a coverage limitation, never evidence of full coverage.
   A contingent allowance of at most600 additional seconds performs basin-centered
   four-variable Sobol sampling only if a discovered candidate improves whole
   development score by >2%, or mean first-error coverage by >15 points with
   whole score<=115% baseline. This rule is fixed before comparison evaluation;
   it tests coupled variables around selected promising basins. It does not
   restart the broad campaign or use comparison outcomes.
6. All feasible candidates retain the entire necklace, verified native first hits,
   native reflection centroids, full spatial contact screening and the corrected
   treatment of unknown rays. Invalid contact certificates are distinguished
   from witnessed penetration and from valid but photographically poor models.
7. Six original first-error/neighbor interiors retain all their native pixels.
   Track best isolated overlap, modal visible overlap, occlusion and competing
   owners. These do not create physical bead centers or verified identities.
   Every candidate also receives the whole-circuit development objective.
8. Finalists are selected before comparison outcomes are exposed. Include a
   global winner and a first-error tradeoff per principal hypothesis where
   distinct; restrict patch-directed ranking to candidates within 15% of their
   baseline whole-circuit score. Validate dense development and retrospective
   comparison, complete supported external contours, background/foreground,
   reflection RMS/P95/max using fixed D=36.878px, missingness and fixed-owner
   controls. A local gain or reassignment-only gain cannot justify adoption.
9. Parameter limits must distinguish physical domains from engineering boxes.
   Exact physical bounds are not measured for most parameters. The finite R311
   exploration box expands several prior numerical bounds; contact, camera and
   construction checks remain hard physical/validity constraints. Fields stay
   byte-identical to each parent; this is not a complete test of every existing
   field, light or axis degree of freedom.
10. Autonomous checkpoint/resume, orphan-trial recovery, source/evidence hashes,
    deterministic proposal identities, signal handling, duplicate avoidance,
    coverage plots, basin records, predicted/measured changes and stopping
    decisions are required. Preserve useful feasible islands across invalid gaps.

Implementation choices: rectangular response maps show unsupported/invalid cells
explicitly; low-discrepancy sampling tests genuinely joint hypotheses; adaptive
subdivision refines promising pair cells without a differentiability assumption.
These three complementary methods address the requested scope without adding a
new physical law. No new synthetic recovery campaign is claimed; native versus
original-kernel replay and analytic contact-witness checks validate computation.

Substantial repair is a prespecified review criterion: at least +15 percentage
points modal coverage at both C-1477-0290 and C-1608-0253, reference coverage no
more than 5 points worse, whole-circuit ownership +2 points and reflection RMS
at least 5% better, with supported boundary RMS, paper occupation, foreground
omission, fixed-owner penalized RMS and missingness no worse (tolerances .02D,
1 point, 1 point, .02D and zero additional missing strong observations).
Every metric remains visible even when this combined criterion fails. Historical
comparison data are already exposed and are not fresh independent validation.

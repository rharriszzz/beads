# R293 development decision — before reserved outcomes

The frozen24 new development targets emphasize well-observed centerline context and
include red/yellow-context and neutral/reflection-like observations. Native-photo
comparison sheets show useful independent bright patches and many usable visible-region
proposals. Clear same-color mergers remain (for example central-0345-1501 and
central-0949-2513), alongside a red neighboring extension at central-0999-0350.
Some colored proposals conservatively retain illuminated material rather than a full
physical silhouette. Reflection outlines remain independent intensity measurements.
These are analyst photographic assessments, not new user judgments or exact truth.

Decision: keep the existing frozen R292 detector unchanged. A small cosmetic mask repair
would not resolve ownership, and another large detector revision is outside the refocused
priority. Separate experimentally useful independent feature measurements and previously
accepted visible regions from unverified full candidate masks. Preserve all alternatives,
partial arcs, uncertainties and clear central failures in the coverage assessment and
short review. No automatic candidate-to-truth promotion, geometric rejection or production
replacement. Phase 2 readiness is conditional on observation type/validation, not silhouette
completion or feature counts. Freeze this decision and source provenance before opening
the24 reserved targets. Do not tune against their outcomes.

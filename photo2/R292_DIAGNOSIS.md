# R292 causal diagnosis and retained failures

Completed human evidence: 13/14 selected R291 Start Here cases accepted, with Q14
revised from A to B and one wrong-clear failure. Full ledger and immutable snapshots
are preserved in R291. No new exact pixel truth or pair-ownership labels exist.

R291's protected-peer test fixes one competing bright center to non-target; it does
not constrain the measured separator. In reserved-yellow-05 the original mask
contains three of five measured trough samples despite excluding the peer center.
This motivates actual arc constraints, not geometry or more point-exclusion rules.

The exposed-case 2x2 ablation exactly replays A and B. Darkness relaxation alone
reproduces preferred B for Q14 and development-yellow-03; weaker peer exclusion has
no effect there. Darkness-only expands accepted red-worst substantially. Therefore
local interruption context is needed rather than globally selecting softer B.

Retained first development approach: `checkpoints/valley-only-v1/` has its sources
and full68 outcomes. It excludes measured dark pixels, but regions can still route
around unresolved ends, and continuation could accept changes with zero supported
edge gain. The second probe adds coherent illumination/material continuation of
observed seams; `checkpoints/illumination-extension-v2/` preserves outcomes/sources.
It revealed that a pure illumination track could start without a valid local dark
anchor. Final v3 requires a bilateral dark anchor, records core contradictions,
requires affirmative supported-edge gain for local material continuation and reports
paired-bank connections in addition to direct valley crossings.

Final general mechanisms and limits:

- Observed-valley plus locally tracked multiscale/color evidence constrains actual
  separator pixels and their peer-facing bank. Unsupported endings are unresolved.
- Darkness connected outside the current initial proposal supports an external
  interpretation. Enclosed darkness and bright-core contradiction remain alternatives.
  This heuristic is dependent on an imperfect proposal; connected internal shading
  can still be mislabeled. No certified physical-hole truth exists in this collection.
- Direct crossings disappear where enforced, but both banks can remain inside by
  routing around an end. Count both, rather than declaring zero mergers.
- Local continuation reproduces accepted B and prevents the large red-worst B
  extension, yet development-red-06 still expands into neighboring material. Material
  and illumination alone do not establish physical ownership.
- Reflection regions can be useful while black-body silhouettes remain unknown.
  Neutral/tinted reflections can corrupt local material estimates and admit paper.
- All 13 accepted references remain exact or within the predeclared review tolerance;
  that tolerance is for avoiding repeat questions, not proof of unchanged correctness.
- Frozen holdouts retain mergers and unstable reflections. Their outcomes were not
  repaired. Failure does not establish insufficient photographic information.

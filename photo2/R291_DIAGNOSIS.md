# R291 diagnosis before detector implementation

Only R290 development results were inspected for this diagnosis. Ten new real-photo
targets were frozen first in r291/evidence/collection.json. The old reserved cases
are retrospective and will not be used as new validation. All old files/feedback
have preservation hashes.

R290's harmonic potential measures connection to a source and a distant frame,
not ownership of one bead. Bright detours defeat incomplete separators. N06 grows
to3458px and includes five other features despite98.6% supported outer sides;
R215-A grows to2797px with seven other features and94.8% outer support. A perimeter
around several beads can be well supported. Those fractions are not accuracy.

P2 has764px, one internal-valley diagnostic, minimum perturbation/context IoU.295
and20.9% supported sides. P5 has878px and IoU.538. Changing the frame changes the
potential even without immediate frame contact. Stable local evidence and unstable
ownership were conflated: the local edge should survive, with uncertainty separate.

Several nominal red-context targets actually highlight dark surfaces; for example
development-red-01 is61px with two bright features. Context-color labels are not
material truth. Expanding every neutral highlight into a neighboring colored bead
would be wrong. Conversely, the bright patch should not delimit the broad diffuse
material visible around P2, N07, N11 and Q14.

R286 established that dark direct profiles can coexist with bright detours; marker
deletion also caused P5 mergers. R288's multiple parallel profiles and affirmative
separation evidence are useful, but short interfaces and internal shading remain
ambiguous. R265's first-dark principle can provide source-relative evidence without
assuming every dark crossing is the external surface or filling holes.

R291 will therefore measure complementary local fields, use target-specific material
continuation and direct-path dark evidence, and protect coherently separated competing
features in one binary target solve. Multiscale illumination/color edges may support
different contour portions. Junction/termination measurements retain occlusion and
internal-shadow ambiguity. A meaningful conflicting-separator alternative is retained,
not selected by visual attractiveness. No shape/area acceptance rule or reviewed
coordinate enters inference. No method failure will itself assert insufficient photo.

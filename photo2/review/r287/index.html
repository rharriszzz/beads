Why brightness ratio alone failed

The preserved first policy abstained on P2 because diffuse red was darker than its highlight. The revised direct-route test allows decreasing brightness while rejecting a valley followed by a renewed bright surface. Identical seam/internal-texture pixels remain physically ambiguous. Narrow bright material is not removed merely for width.

Black remains unchanged R2 reflection-only measurement, with R1/R4 alternatives and unknown physical ownership. No dark-body inference or new reflection tuning.

Recommendation and stop

Keep the direct diffuse-continuation and multi-path seam diagnostics for further review. Do not adopt the frozen selector unchanged: “M unstable” is not proof that “J adequate.” No hybrid was justified. Await user assessment before another bounded experiment or adoption; no R288, global physical fitting or pattern search has begun.

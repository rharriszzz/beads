# R293 Phase 1 priority policy

The permanent PHASE1_MEASUREMENTS.md contract remains in force and production is
unchanged. This experimental policy corrects selection/review priorities, not bead
shape requirements.

1. Start with independent well-illuminated photographic observations along the existing
   unregistered image-derived centerline and nearby. R275's26px point reach and40px
   nearby scan reach are accounting/search conventions, never object acceptance bounds.
   Measured visible supports can also intersect the core band. No fixed bead quota or
   one-feature-per-station interpretation is permitted.
2. Include both signed sides and distinguish multiple photographed surfaces when
   separation evidence supports them. Keep duplicate/highlight ownership and competing
   curve-branch interpretations unresolved. Signed side is local orientation, not a
   global necklace arrangement inside/outside or physical index.
3. Red/yellow observations retain independent bright patches and supported visible
   material, representative positions and uncertainty. Full physical silhouettes are
   unnecessary. Original bright feature, patch centroid, candidate region centroid and
   physical center remain separate; the last is unknown in Phase 1.
4. Black measurements remain independent illuminated reflection regions only. No dark
   collar/body reconstruction or physical-center conversion. Neutral/colored reflection
   context is imperfect: retain original RGB, source labels and unknown physical owner
   rather than recoloring source observations.
5. Distinguish clear central measurement failures from lower-priority weak/peripheral
   uncertainty. Distance to photograph border and outside of the necklace arrangement
   are different spatial descriptors. Neither certifies photographic quality. A clear
   outside observation remains useful; a poor central one is a central gap. Defer difficult
   full peripheral silhouettes, preserving their independent evidence and uncertainty.
6. Illumination/contrast/resolution proxies select the evaluation population; they are
   not calibrated correctness, ownership or visibility estimates. The new sample is
   balanced geographically and by source context/side, not population-proportional.
   Historical difficult cases remain separate regressions and are not the progress
   denominator. Reserve new outcomes before decisions and never tune against them.
7. Keep unchanged accepted/reviewed cases read-only in the new experiment. Ask6–10
   new, informative central questions with native raw photo, automatic proposals and
   optional diagnostics. No tracing, masks or repeat ownership labor.
8. Prepare experimental linked observations for conditional Phase 2 use: original pixels,
   peak/patch position, intensity/shape, accepted visible measurements and separate
   unverified masks/partial arcs. Physical identities, centers and bead counts remain
   unknown. Phase 2/3 must never manufacture, move, recolor or replace Phase 1 records.
   No production adoption or reconstruction occurs in R293.

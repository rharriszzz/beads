# R309 frozen experiment design

One continuous R306 geometric necklace, original photographic reference untouched.
All inherited32-node fields are frozen. No regional optimization or per-body motion.
Training uses the existing24 geographic bands and comparison the8 remaining bands;
both cover the circuit. Comparison has historical exposure, not fresh validation.

D is the longest of16 independently digitized R298 visible contours: B14,
Feret major-axis distance36.87817782917155px, endpoints retained in evidence/D.json.
This is a fixed reference, not an established maximum over all beads. ±3px boundary
uncertainty at each end implies conservative±6px length uncertainty. Areas remain
percentages, never divided by this linear dimension.

All four objectives receive identical starts and a shared response pool. Starts
include R3062405/hand−/open,2733/hand−/closed,2204/hand+/closed, R307 and R308.
Shared native probes (up to30% of1200 seconds): full2pi phase, longitudinal±12px,
turn±.005 where open, counts±1/±13, and globally acting height/projection/light laws.
Remaining time split equally between reflection, boundary, ownership and balanced
branches. Each branch may select from the SAME shared feasible hypotheses; its
selected basin need not be the same. Actual time/evaluations and cache reuse saved.
Branch numerical derivatives require second/first difference ratio<.35 on all
active columns. Otherwise retain bounded coordinate bracket trials; no unstable
Jacobian descent. Stop after eight stagnant blocks or the time bound. SIGTERM,
source/evidence fingerprints, atomic trials, resume and native caching supported.

Every objective uses actual1px first-hit predictions and full-chain separation.
No coarse optics ranking. Approximately3–6 globally acting variables per block.
Unknown rays cannot create separator/contour edges or earn empty-paper credit.
Shared source illumination, no per-bead lights or colors. Finite light uses one
point source at distance1/light_inverse along the shared direction, inverse-square
attenuation, surface normals and all-body segment shadow tests.

Height coordinate y=(photographic geometric-axis height−1591)/1591. A minor-radius
factor1+minor_y*y (|minor_y|≤.04), orientation tilt_y*y+tilt_y2*(y²−1/3) degrees
(each coefficient≤3°), and existing perspective≤.001 are separate explanations.
These are conservative exploratory laws: at typical minor4.2,2% changes radius
by.084 source units (~.047 bead radii);1° tilt changes a body edge ~.016 radii.
No claim these coefficients are measured physical strain or camera calibration.
They apply continuously everywhere, including both sides at equal heights.

Reflection fitting uses unthresholded visible specular intensity: Gaussian overlap
with an observation (sigma=D/3), converted to sqrt(−2sigma²log overlap). This is a
smooth positional surrogate, NOT the exact thresholded-centroid displacement.
Zero intensity incurs2D; exact missing lobes carry an additional penalty and stay
in final denominators. Exact0.5-peak centroids,0.25/0.75 controls and0.5px sampling
remain independent numerical diagnostics. Surface-compatible one-to-one strong
assignments and frozen-owner controls retain alternatives and missingness.

Distances are normalized by fixedD. Tail loss is mean(e²)+.5*sqrt(mean(e⁴));
uncertain contours use a3px band for fitting and raw bidirectional distances for
reporting. Contours/regions count as units, not thousands of independent pixels.
Boundary objective also includes supported separator and envelope evidence.
Ownership uses confidence-weighted modal deficits and cross-color contradictions;
same-color possible mergers remain weak ownership evidence. Each single-emphasis
objective adds.15 material and.25 missing-lobe terms. Balanced sums the four
components plus missingness. Full score definitions are in measure.py.

Acceptance independent of weighted loss: versus R306, R307 and R308 require≥5%
reliable centroid RMS and bidirectional raw boundary RMS improvement,≥2 percentage
points ownership gain, no extra missing reliable reflections, maximum errors no
worse by>.05D, paper/foreground regressions≤1percentage point. Region checks:
no coherent-ownership loss>10pp, background increase>5pp or strong RMS increase>3px
in sufficiently measured geographic bands. These are engineering gates, not tests
of statistical significance. Absolute maxima and reliable maxima stay explicit.

Whole-circuit residuals, top/bottom and both-side diagnostics, all objectives on
all outcomes, native finals, original-Python checks, same-scale50-bead atlas and
browser tests are delivered even if no candidate passes. No Phase3 or patterns.

Final-measurement clarification before any R309 final comparison: the16 observed
polygons annotate external visible silhouettes, without measured internal hole
contours. Final supported-boundary distances therefore compare complete external
native silhouettes in both directions. Internal hole segments remain in the native
viewer and are counted as unmeasured by this photographic contour evidence. The
inherited fitting contour term includes all visible edges inside its ROI; preserve
that competing measurement separately to test whether it predicts the independent
external-boundary result. No fitting source, score, parameter or budget is changed.

Native final selection, specified before R309 comparison evaluation: reevaluate the
two best unique already-completed settings plus the accepted incumbent for each
objective, with free compatible owners on identical native training evidence. This
includes completed proposals from a budget-interrupted last numerical block. No new
parameters are proposed. Charge and report this small ranking audit separately;
freeze objective winners before retrospective comparison. Diagnostic height-zero
counterfactuals cannot displace those winners.

The height-orientation law rotates the bead axis toward the local helix radial
direction, unlike R306's shared circumferential tilt. This is an empirical small
orientation control with full contact checks, not a measured thread mechanics law.
The finite source is located relative to the global model center, with one common
position and all-body shadows; no per-body lighting corrections are permitted.

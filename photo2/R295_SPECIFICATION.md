## Diagnosis before implementation

R294's objective rewards maximum first-hit patch containment independently of
material ownership. Numerous correlated highlights can outweigh one large region.
Its one-to-one automatic-interior assignment assumes more ownership than those
masks establish, yet still accepts partial overlaps. It does not penalize modeled
boundaries cutting illuminated material or conflicting colors on one owner.
The paper penalty is weak and physical feasibility is selected only after fitting.
Thus correspondence/objective failure is demonstrated; geometry failure is plausible
but not isolated. Camera elevation, scale, bead axial thickness and spacing are
coupled. Small smooth centerline changes cannot repair a wrong local lattice.
Reflection flags need not represent mirror highlights, so normal/light inconsistency
can reflect ownership, surface geometry, extended illumination or flag errors.

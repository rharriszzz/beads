# R289 — independent photographic landmarks

Authorized by the R289 request, superseding the R288 stop for this experiment.
No production changes, global photographic pose/light refit, indexing or patterns.
Deliver scoped code, reproducible data, raw/marked crops, tests, commit, push and
remote verification; then stop for review. Retain all failures and duplicate cues.

1. Recompute R276 observations and the complete highlight-relative-interior ledger
   from R275 pixels/masks, without rewriting old files. Reproduce constant-vector
   calibration on its original training/holdout split. That calibration is a
   retrospective baseline, not independent confirmation or physical-center truth.
2. Detect local luminance maxima over the entire original ICC-managed photograph.
   Only RGB enters inference. Compare native maximum, connected small bright patch,
   and patch weighted by luminance above a local background. Retain every plausible
   maximum; do not replace an excluded seed with a nearby component. Record patch
   width/orientation, domain contact, sensitivity and possible duplicate groups.
3. Fit on spatially separated geographic development regions; freeze before
   evaluation. Compare constant displacement with a single geographic harmonic and
   measured patch-axis dependence. Use R275 conservative interiors as broad proxy
   references and saved J/W as separate references, explicitly imperfect. Matching
   to masks happens only after independent detection; record ambiguity and missing
   matches. No nearest-bead relocation, unseen truth or masks enter detection.
4. Test one darkness method: horizontal and vertical short profiles with five
   parallel native samples, first coherent low transition on each side. No detour
   can cancel a stop. Missing/opposed edges stay missing. Test a single linear
   refinement; no retuning or special cases after heldout evaluation.
5. Fresh rounded-annular POV scenes with identical bead dimensions across colors,
   varied pose, occlusion and illumination, paired specular on/off. Truth read only
   after inference. Measure visible centroid, projected center and isolated specular
   contribution. Compare empirical landmark offsets and known-geometry normal-based
   reflection prediction. No global photograph fit. Distinguish measurement,
   correspondence, material/light and pose failures.
6. Median/RMS/p90/p95 native pixel errors and diameter fractions, by color and
   geography; all denominators and failures; recommendations for Phase 2 point,
   uncertainty and essential features. Real fractions use an explicit nominal
   projected diameter proxy, never claim measured physical diameter.

Predeclared practical improvement: >=10% RMS reduction without worse p90 on
heldout paired cases. Simpler measurement preferred otherwise. No extra families.

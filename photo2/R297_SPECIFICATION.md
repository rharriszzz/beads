Approved: debug and strengthen the standalone whole-necklace Phase 2 optimizer,
retain competing count/hand/topology families and physical gating, perform bounded
experiments, deliver an offline photographic debugger and stop for review.

All R294–R296 artifacts, Phase 1 observations and beads.pov are protected by
`r297/evidence/preservation.json.gz`. Only the standalone launcher changes outside
R297 code/results/docs and handoff metadata. No pattern inference or adoption.

The R296 train/validation split remains development evidence. Its previously
revealed reserved intervals are retrospective final checks only. They will remain
excluded from the R297 objective and final pilot selection, and will be re-evaluated
only after freezing final parameters. All eligible material has already been used
or inspected by R296; a new geographic split cannot provide untouched information.
No new-image accuracy or independent physical-identity claim is possible here.
Native supports, not sampled scores, determine reported photographic comparisons.

Diagnosis: separate placement normalization, minor winding, camera, shape, local
fields and front-surface assignment effects using paired controls. Compare every
retained hand/topology family and the original local R295 geometry. A model with
inconclusive contact is never selectable; only actual overlap establishes collision.

Development: compare the original objective and section-balanced substantial-region
objective using equal proposal counts and identical starts. Ablate patch and
boundary terms. Preserve all outcomes and source versions. Then one medium run
(up to 512 trials) and one final pilot (up to 768 trials, 30 minutes search cap).
Smoke/restart and full-loop contact/seam tests precede the final pilot. Further
corrections require development evidence, and any prior run stays immutable.

The new debugger will expose all 32 geographic sections, each original measured
support and its alternatives, material/edge/paper/color disagreements, model-only
sites, visibility, index drift, seam and contact witnesses. Numerical indicators
remain fallible diagnostic evidence; photographed irregularity alone is no rejection.

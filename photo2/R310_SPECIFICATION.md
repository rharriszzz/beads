# R310 diagnostic scope and measurement policy

User priority is first outward positioning failure, then curvature. No geometric
parameters or fitted fields may change. Models: unchanged R306 primary2405,
R309 balanced2733, and R2972337 because it has stronger anchor-interior coverage.
Anchor is photographic C-1497-0271, not assumed universal index0.

All available saved positive nonreflection interiors are inspected. Both directions
include prefixes10,20,40,80,160,320 and automatic full-circuit geographic scanning.
Conditional origins come from dominant native ownership of the anchor. Competing
owners survive; no physical sequence is asserted. Suspected fragmentation is<80%
coherence. The stricter material discrepancy rule uses<65% best core ownership,
>=20% eroded core farther than0.15D from its whole visible modal surface, and>=40
core pixels. Erosion0/1/2/3 and distance0.1/0.15/0.2D sensitivity is reported.
This is not a physical-center displacement threshold or calibrated confidence test.
Unknown rays retain uncertainty. Reflection offsets are exact Euclidean pairs,
with signed photographic tangent/normal components; weak and missing observations
remain available. Absolute pixel and D-normalized distance statistics are saved.

Visible versus isolated projections test neighbor occlusion. A fixed-geometry
one-to-one assignment checks whether relabeling alone improves the near-anchor
supports. This is read-only correspondence diagnosis, not patchwise fitting.
Observed centroid neighbor vectors remain image proxies unless physical identity
and centers are independently supported; historical dark separators cannot certify
+1/+6/+7 integer neighbors. No new human labels are inferred.

Only after the saved Part A completion record: verify planar bending derivatives,
frames and center reproduction; use analytic circles to test inner/outer compression;
relate existing full-model curvature/foreshortening to photographic discrepancies.
A hypothetical future curvature law may be described but never applied.

Historical masks may merge same-color bodies. Therefore the report distinguishes
first suspected fragmentation, substantial material inconsistency, sustained nearby
corroboration and an unestablished first physical positioning failure. No threshold
is used to pretend that earlier ambiguous or unsupported bodies were correct.
